## Getting started

You need three things: a 3D model (from CAD software, or downloaded), a *slicer* that turns the model into instructions for the printer, and a printer with material.

Most printers on this site print plastic filament (FDM). They are forgiving machines and a good first step into digital fabrication. Resin printers give finer detail, but need more care and protective gear.

## Good to know

- Printing takes hours, not minutes. Plan ahead, especially near deadlines.
- Ask the staff of the makerspace which slicer settings and materials they use. It saves a lot of failed prints.
- Design for the process: think about overhangs, wall thickness and which side lies on the bed.
