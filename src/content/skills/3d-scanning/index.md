## Getting started

A 3D scanner measures the surface of an object and turns it into a digital model that you can measure, edit or print. It is used to design products that fit the human body, to copy existing parts and to document prototypes.

## Good to know

- Scans nearly always need cleaning up in software before you can use them.
- Shiny, transparent and very dark surfaces are hard to scan.
- When you scan people, ask for their permission and treat the data with care.
