## Getting started

CNC milling starts with a 3D or 2D design and *CAM* software, which works out the path the cutter has to follow. It gives you strong, precise parts in real materials, but it takes more preparation than 3D printing.

## Good to know

- CNC machines are powerful and unforgiving. Expect to need training or to work together with the staff.
- Fixing the workpiece properly matters as much as the program.
- Start with foam or wood before you move on to metal.
