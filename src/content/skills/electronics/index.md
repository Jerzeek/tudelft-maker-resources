## Getting started

Most interactive prototypes need some electronics: a microcontroller, a few sensors, something that moves or lights up. Start on a breadboard, and solder it once it works.

## Good to know

- Several makerspaces lend out components and development kits, so ask before you buy.
- Measuring equipment such as multimeters and oscilloscopes helps you find out why something does not work.
- For anything connected to mains voltage or large batteries, ask an expert first.
