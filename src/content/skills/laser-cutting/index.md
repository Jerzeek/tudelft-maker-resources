## Getting started

A laser cutter follows the lines of a vector drawing (made in for example Illustrator, Inkscape or a CAD program) and cuts or engraves sheet material such as wood, cardboard and acrylic.

## Good to know

- Every makerspace has its own rules about which materials are allowed. Some plastics give off toxic fumes: always check first.
- Most places ask you to follow a short instruction before you may use the machine yourself.
- Bring your file in the format the makerspace asks for, and bring or buy the right sheet size.
