## Getting started

Working with metal covers a family of techniques: turning on a lathe, milling, drilling, cutting and bending sheet metal, and welding. They are the way to make parts that have to be strong and accurate.

## Good to know

- Machines for metal nearly always require a training workshop first.
- Workshop staff are glad to think along about how a part can best be made. Bring a drawing.
- Safety shoes, safety glasses and a workshop coat are usually mandatory.
