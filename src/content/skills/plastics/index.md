## Getting started

Vacuum forming pulls a heated plastic sheet over a mould and is a quick way to make shells and packaging. Shredding and melting waste plastic turns it into new sheets, beams or parts.

## Good to know

- Know which plastic you are working with. Different types melt at different temperatures, and some should never be heated.
- Work with good ventilation.
