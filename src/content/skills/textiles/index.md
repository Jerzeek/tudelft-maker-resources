## Getting started

From a simple bag to a wearable with built-in electronics: textiles ask for their own tools and skills. Several workshops have sewing machines, and some have embroidery or knitting machines.

## Good to know

- Bring your own fabric and thread unless the workshop sells them.
- Industrial sewing machines are much faster than household ones. Ask for an introduction.
