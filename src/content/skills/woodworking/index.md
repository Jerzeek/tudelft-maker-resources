## Getting started

Wood is one of the most accessible materials for building prototypes and models at full scale. Most workshops have saws, drills and sanders, and staff who can show you how to use them safely.

## Good to know

- You always need a safety instruction before you use woodworking machines.
- Wear the protective gear the workshop asks for, and tie back long hair.
- Bring a drawing with dimensions. It makes asking for advice much easier.
