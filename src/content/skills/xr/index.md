## Getting started

Extended reality (XR) covers virtual, augmented and mixed reality. It lets you walk around a design at full scale, or test an interaction, long before a physical prototype exists.

## Good to know

- You do not have to buy a headset: several places on campus let you try the hardware.
- Game engines such as Unity and Unreal are the usual tools to build your own application.
