The model hall is the central making space in the heart of the Architecture faculty. It is mainly intended for making scale models, and there are multiple woodworking machines. On the ground floor you find the central black desk, worktables, photo booths and the BK Green recycling cabinet. The model hall also contains the wood workshop and the CAMlab.

## Black desk

At the black desk the staff can help you with the following:

- You can borrow tools (your campus card is the deposit). Most tools can be borrowed overnight. Electrical appliances need to be returned each evening and may not leave the model hall.
- You can order from the materials catalogue and buy model-making items such as brushes, scale figures or pieces of wood from the stock.
- Glue, old newspapers, sticks, needles and sandpaper are always available and free of charge.
- You can ask for advice.

## Worktables

All tables on the ground floor are available to everyone on a daily basis. Each table needs to be cleared by its user at the end of the day, so that it is available again the next day. There is no storage in the model hall: everything that is not approved or agreed upon will be discarded by the staff.

Only BK master students who have passed their P4 can reserve a table while they prepare for their final exam, and leave their work on it.

## BK Green cabinet

This cabinet, behind the black desk, contains leftover materials that are free to take. You can also leave your own usable leftovers there (no laser remains, please).

## Photo booths

There are two photo booths, one white and one black. Lights can be borrowed at the CAMlab.

## Wood workshop, CAMlab and mezzanines

The glass structures inside the model hall are the wood workshop and the CAMlab. Looking in the direction of the restaurant, the wood workshop is on your right and the CAMlab on your left.

The wood workshop holds the woodworking machines. You must receive safety instructions from a staff member before you are allowed to operate them. Short videos with user instructions are available online (see the links on this page).

The CAMlab also contains the office of the model hall staff, the paint room with spray booth and two CNC milling machines.

On top of these glass structures are two mezzanines, mostly used for form study classes. They are not for general student use.
