Buurman Rotterdam is a woodworking workshop and materials hub focused on sustainability, circular design and hands-on crafting. It offers reclaimed materials (wood, metal, plastics) and a range of workshops, courses and activities.

If you want to develop practical woodworking skills, Buurman is a great place to get your hands dirty, learn to work with real-world materials and test ideas in a low-cost, sustainable way. You can also take workshops, or work on projects together with other makers, designers and circular economy enthusiasts.
