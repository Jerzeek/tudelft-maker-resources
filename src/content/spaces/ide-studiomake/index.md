StudioMake is part of the StudioLab, a design research community at the IDE faculty of researchers, PhD candidates and graduating MSc students for whom prototyping plays an important role in developing and sharing knowledge. As a student you can use the facilities and materials at StudioMake by agreement.

## Facilities

- A large collection of Seeed Studio Grove and other reusable electronic components, which can be borrowed by agreement.
- A collection of scrap materials for quick-and-dirty prototypes.
- 3D printers, including the multi-material Prusa XL.
- Facilities for working with electronics (soldering stations, PCB ovens, oscilloscopes and more), to be used by agreement.

## Tips

- Discuss your needs with the StudioMake staff and agree on which materials and facilities you can use.
- The room is not accessible when staff is not present.
- The StudioLab floor is a vibrant and informal space, but be respectful of the researchers in the neighbouring studios. They are often doing work that should not be disturbed.
