Makerspace Delft is a non-profit co-working space. Its mission is to make prototyping tools, machinery and mentorship affordable for everyone. The workshop is intended for makers who start small or individual engineering projects.

It is located in the centre of Delft and offers a different setting, vibe and community than the makerspaces on the TU Delft campus.

- 3D printers, a laser cutter, soldering stations and power tools
- Membership of €10 for 5 hours per month, or €25 for 50 hours per month
- A network of local makers
