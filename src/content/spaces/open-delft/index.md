If you are looking for a place with guidance to make things over a longer period (half a year to a year), you can sign up for the courses of the public library of Delft. Not the university library. Think of ceramics, woodworking or screen printing.

It is cheaper than X and more of a commitment, but the personal guidance and the techniques you learn are far more valuable. It also takes you out of the student bubble: most participants are older residents of Delft.
