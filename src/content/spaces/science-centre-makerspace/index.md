The Inventory Makerspace is part of the TU Delft Science Centre. It aims to give TU Delft students a place to gain practical engineering experience. It also hosts the Science Centre's own activities, such as workshops for primary and secondary schools.

## Facilities

- A large project room with worktables
- Laser cutters
- Woodworking machines
- A wide range of tools
- 3D printers

## Practicalities

- Most machines at this makerspace can be used directly, including the laser cutters, which allows for quick trial-and-error experiments. They do require training first. The training can be arranged via X, the sports and culture centre of TU Delft.
- Once the training is done, you get access to the machines you are entitled to use by swiping your campus card.
- As a student, you pay for the material you use. Materials are available on location. Using your own materials needs to be discussed with the makerspace staff.
- The makerspace is open to students who want to work on personal projects, student teams, artists, course projects and youth workshops. Check the availability with the makerspace staff: organised activities get priority, and you may not be able to work there during time slots booked by others.
