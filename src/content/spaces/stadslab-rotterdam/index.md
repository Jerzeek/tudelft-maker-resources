Stadslab Rotterdam is the workshop of CMI, the Institute for Communication, Media and Information Technology of Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences. It is not large, but it has many machines, such as laser cutters, 3D printers and a CNC embroidery machine. There are also facilities for working with virtual and augmented reality. It is an official FabLab, which means it is open to the general public.

This makerspace has a very hands-on and informal attitude: machines can be operated directly by the makers. It is run mainly by students of the university of applied sciences, has a fun, informal vibe, and is home to a vibrant community of makers. It can get very busy on some days, and education that runs there always has priority.

## Facilities

- 3D printers
- Laser cutters
- CNC embroidery machine
- Vacuum former
- Vinyl cutter
- Poster printer
- CNC mill
- Plastic recycling
- VR goggles
