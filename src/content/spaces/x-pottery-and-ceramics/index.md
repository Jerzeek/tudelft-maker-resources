The Open Studios for pottery and ceramics are open to anyone with an X subscription who has completed a Pottery or Ceramics course, and to anyone with experience in ceramics, at any time when an assistant is present. The assistant helps when needed, gives tips and advice, and makes sure that materials and equipment are used responsibly and safely. Register via the X website, under "Ceramics".

- In the **Open Studio: Pottery** you can use the potter's wheels and other tools.
- In the **Open Studio: Ceramics** you can use the techniques, tools and materials that you were taught in the ceramics lessons.

Clay, glazes, clay slip, pigments and plaster are for sale.

## Courses

To follow a course you pay a fee, but you do not need an X subscription.

- **Ceramics.** Clay is shaped, decorated, glazed and fired in a kiln. Depending on the clay and the firing temperature, the result is porcelain, stoneware or earthenware.
- **Pottery.** Throwing clay on a turning wheel to make cups, bowls and vases. It takes a lot of practice, and lets you develop a style of your own.
