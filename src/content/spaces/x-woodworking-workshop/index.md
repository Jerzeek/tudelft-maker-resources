The Open Studio: Woodworking is open to anyone with an X subscription who has completed a Woodworking or Furniture Design course, and to anyone with woodworking experience, at any time when an assistant is present. All you need to do is register beforehand.

The assistant is there to help when needed, to give tips and advice, and to make sure that materials and equipment are used responsibly and safely.

During the open studio you are welcome to use hand tools and small machines. Materials are for sale, or you can bring your own.

## Courses

To follow a course you pay a fee, but you do not need an X subscription.

- **Furniture Design.** Design and build your own piece of furniture.
- **Wood Carving.** Become confident with hand tools for carving, choosing wood and transferring your design onto it.
