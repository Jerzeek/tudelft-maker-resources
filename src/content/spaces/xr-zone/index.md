The XR Zone is a central TU Delft facility that supports using, building and exploring with XR (extended reality), including virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality and 3D game engines. It is located in the TU Delft Library, to the far right of the main entrance.

You are always welcome to get personal guidance for your project, to test the latest hardware and software, or just to explore existing VR solutions for research and learning.

## Service

- Support in building your VR, AR, XR or 3D application. (They will not build it for you.)
- Support for working with Unreal Engine and Unity.
- Use of hardware, software and support is free.
- No appointments: just visit the XR Zone in the Library.

## Rules

- First come, first served. When capacity is reached, your project will have to wait.
- Respect the hardware and the employees.
- There is no booking system. Your project is discussed and planned when you get there.
- All hardware stays in the XR Zone. If you need equipment on location, for a demo for example, this can be discussed.
- You are responsible for your own data. Back up your work before 17:00, on a portable SSD or a cloud service.
- Feel free to walk around and ask anything.
