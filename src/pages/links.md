This guide is about hands-on making. For research instruments and specialised labs, these overviews go further.

## At TU Delft

- [Find equipment — TU Delft Research Portal](https://research.tudelft.nl/en/equipments/). Research equipment and facilities, searchable by department.
- [Research facilities at TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/onderzoek/onderzoeksfaciliteiten). The large research facilities of the university, some of them unique in the Netherlands.
- [PMB facilities at Industrial Design Engineering](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/ide/about-ide/facilities/pmb/facilities). The official list of machines in the IDE model making workshop.

## In the Netherlands

- [4TU research facilities for materials scientists](https://www.4tu.nl/htm/research/research-facilities/). A survey of equipment and experiments in the laboratories of the four technical universities.

## Inspiration

- [MIT Project Manus](https://make.mit.edu/). The overview of makerspaces, equipment and training at MIT that inspired this guide.
- [UTA FabLab open tools](https://github.com/UTA-FabLab). Open-source software written by student employees of the University of Texas at Arlington FabLab to run a makerspace.
